01 / About

AI is changing every field. Everyone should have a seat at the table.

We bring University of Alberta students together to explore AI through practical, approachable experiences. You do not need to study computing science or arrive with a project idea.

Come to learn a workflow, meet a collaborator, pressure-test an idea, or simply ask better questions about where this technology is going.
